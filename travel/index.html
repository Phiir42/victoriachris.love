Getting there

By car

Gloucester is on Cape Ann, about an hour’s drive from Cambridge and Boston.

Rome2Rio gives about 52 minutes from Cambridge without traffic; checked November 22, 2026.

By air

The nearest major airport is Boston Logan International Airport (BOS).

Discover Gloucester (discovergloucester.com), checked November 25, 2026.

Parking

There is a parking lot at the castle.

Uber

Uber is usually easy to get, but cars can be hard to find in the evening and at night. If you plan to leave by Uber, allow extra time.

Local taxi

Gloucester Taxi & Livery Service, 978-283-0099. The castle’s vendor list says cash only.

From the vendor list Hammond Castle gave us. We have not rechecked it; call ahead.
