What the form collects

Draft. We will check this list against the final form before RSVPs open.

The form is run by RSVPify, an outside RSVP service. It asks for:

  • Your name and whether each guest in your household will attend
  • Each guest’s entrée choice, allergies and dietary restrictions
  • Whether your household will ride the shuttle
  • Optional: accessibility needs and a song request

Your answers go to RSVPify and to us, Victoria and Chris. We share meal choices and allergies with our caterer, and the shuttle count with the bus company.
