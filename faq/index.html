Questions & Answers

Short answers to what guests ask most.

When and where is the wedding?

Saturday, August 28, 2027, at Hammond Castle Museum, 80 Hesperus Avenue, Gloucester, MA 01930. The ceremony and reception are both at the castle.

What time should I arrive?

Please arrive by 5:00 PM Eastern Time. The ceremony starts at 5:15 PM. The evening ends at 10:00 PM.

See the schedule

What should I wear?

Formal attire. Themed medieval attire is also welcome, as long as it suits a formal evening.

Can I bring a guest?

Invitations are for the guests named on them.

Are children invited?

The celebration is for adults only. For young children under 5 there will be a children’s corner with childcare. Write to us for details.

How do I RSVP?

On the RSVP page. The reply-by date will be on that page.

Go to RSVP

Is there a shuttle?

Yes. It leaves the Kimpton Marlowe Hotel in Cambridge at 3:45 PM Eastern Time and leaves the castle at 10:30 PM. There is one return trip. The return trip drops off at the Kimpton Marlowe Hotel and at the Holiday Inn Express & Suites Boston-Cambridge. No alcohol on the bus. Tell us on your RSVP if you will ride.

Shuttle details

Where should I stay?

We have room blocks at two hotels in Cambridge: the Kimpton Marlowe Hotel, where the shuttle picks up, and the Holiday Inn Express & Suites Boston-Cambridge. There are also inns and hotels near the castle.

Hotels and travel

Is there parking at the castle?

Yes. There is a parking lot at the castle. Uber is usually easy to get, but cars can be hard to find in the evening and at night. If you plan to leave by Uber, allow extra time.

What is for dinner?

A plated dinner. You choose an entrée when you RSVP: pan-seared tuscan chicken, cabernet-braised boneless short ribs, or mediterranean vegetable ravioli (vegetarian / vegan, on request).

I have a food allergy. What should I do?

The menu is written as peanut-free. The caterer’s kitchen is not tree-nut-free. If you have an allergy or a dietary restriction, tell us on your RSVP.
